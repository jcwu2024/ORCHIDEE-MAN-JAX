Old pilot, failed/rejected architecture, checkpoint continuation, launch,
profiling and custom-reverse reports remain evidence under their own scope.

proposals do not transfer authorization to a new job or architecture.
Use [HISTORY_INDEX.md](research/daily_coarse_graining/HISTORY_INDEX.md) for a
small question-routed evidence selection and exact pre-cleanup read commands.

Do not update every old report to repeat current status. Update current facts
here/elsewhere once and link the relevant immutable evidence. Add a new dated
report only when there is genuinely new evidence or a consequential decision.
