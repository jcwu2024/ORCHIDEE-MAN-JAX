## Why the current four arms are insufficient

Their common starting point is fresh joint-training step4000, followed by20
updates with added litter supervision, followed by80 more updates. All four
inherit parameters AND AdamW moments at absolute4100. Matching that starting
state controls a local comparison but does not remove path dependence.
Neither its winning coefficient nor its learning-rate ranking can be assumed
to hold at random initialization. These arms diagnose useful directions;
they cannot supply the final paper checkpoint merely through further tuning.

The user reaffirmed this distinction on2026-09-20. The next learning-method
verification should start from declared random initialization and fresh
optimizer state. Do not turn another inherited-weight sweep into the default
next workstream. Current results and checkpoints remain diagnostic assets.

## How to use array15290789 results

1. Collect all completed100/250/500 NEW-update assessments and actual runtime.
   Separate completed, wall-limited, physical-error and science-stopped arms.
   Compare arms at COMMON completed milestones, not unequal training lengths.
2. Compare physical-unit GPP/NPP/RA, AGB/BGB growth, canopy/nonliving changes,
   signed bias and wrong-sign counts. Do not compare different weighted total
   losses as an accuracy ranking. Preserve per-window results and teacher
   magnitudes; tiny or zero targets make naive relative errors misleading.
3. Use the100/250/500 trajectories to distinguish transient effects from
   sustained tradeoffs. Keep a small set of nondominated settings; conflicting
   outcomes are not evidence that the smallest total loss is best.
4. Freeze at most TWO justified fresh-start recipes before any new training.
   Write exact coefficients/LR/schedule, seeds, optimizer, sampling, length,
   milestones and stopping/checkpoint rules into executable configuration.
   The actual settings remain OPEN until the current evidence is collected.
   Do not automatically repeat all four settings or multiply four by many seeds.
5. Verify meaningful fresh-state values/gradients and bounded full updates
   for the effective objective, reusing admitted unchanged AD infrastructure.
   Do not demand trained accuracy from untrained weights or redo historical
   adjoint investigations without a new failure. Coefficients calibrated at
   step4000 are candidate settings, not certified fresh-state gradient balance.
6. Compare paired fresh learning curves, then repeat the leading declared
   method under the second initialization. A second seed is a minimal
   robustness check, not proof of universal stability. If ranking reverses or
   variation dominates the method effect, report that uncertainty; do not
   keep reseeding until a favorable result appears.
